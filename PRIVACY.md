# Chat Cleaner Free Beta — Privacy information

**Beta v1.6.0 · Updated November 2026**

The extension runs on `chatgpt.com` to display selection checkboxes, title filters, and archive/delete controls.

**On your device:** Sidebar chat titles and identifiers are read to make selections. If you choose to import a ChatGPT `conversations.json`, it is processed in the current browser tab for optional searches and isn't uploaded. Optional last-opened dates may be stored locally using Chrome extension storage only if you enable that feature. You can erase that history in the extension.

**ChatGPT actions:** Archive/Delete uses ChatGPT's existing user-facing controls. ChatGPT separately receives and processes those requests.

**Optional feedback:** After a batch ends, you can dismiss the feedback prompt or explicitly submit a **Worked**, **Had an issue**, or **Suggestion** rating and optional comment up to 280 characters. Submitting sends the feedback through the third-party FormSubmit service (`formsubmit.co`) to the developer's inbox, **ops.by.amber@gmail.com**. The submission includes the category, extension version, archive/delete action, generic batch outcome, requested item count, observed sidebar-removal count, and the comment you choose to type. It does **not** automatically include chat titles, chat IDs, messages, imported exports, or diagnostic logs. FormSubmit receives ordinary request metadata, such as an IP address, and states that it retains submissions for 30 days. The developer may keep feedback emails for product support and improvement until manually deleted. Feedback delivery requires activation of the recipient inbox; it is not yet verified.

No feedback is sent automatically when a batch finishes. No payments, advertising identifiers, or accounts are collected by the extension. Don't include private chat details in an optional feedback comment or GitHub Issue.

**Control:** Close the prompt to decline sending. Erase locally tracked opened dates in the extension. Remove the extension through Chrome settings to stop operation and clear its local extension data.

**Contact:** ops.by.amber@gmail.com. This beta policy may be revised before Chrome Web Store publication.
